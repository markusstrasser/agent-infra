## Near Misses / Context

- **GTA-2**: https://github.com/open-compass/GTA · 2026-04 release. Strong general-tool-agent suite with GTA-Workflow, but slightly outside the 4-week priority window.
- **AgencyBench V2**: https://github.com/GAIR-NLP/AgencyBench · 2026-01. Important 1M-token/90-tool-call long-horizon benchmark, but not new in May-June.
- **MCP-Atlas / MCP-Universe / MCPVerse / OSWorld-MCP**: useful MCP context, but mostly older than the requested window or primarily leaderboard refreshes.

## TOP STEALS

1. **Adopt trajectory-aware judging** from WeaveBench: judge deliverables + traces + screenshots/logs, not just final files.  
2. **Continuously validate benchmark tasks** like Terminal-Bench 2.1: stale dependencies and mismatched tests are now first-class eval failures.  
3. **Measure model × harness, not model alone** from PawBench/WildClawBench: local Claude Code/Codex/Cursor differences can be as large as a model upgrade.
