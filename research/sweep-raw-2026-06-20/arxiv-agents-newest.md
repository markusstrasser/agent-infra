No strong pre-frontier-only result is doing load-bearing work here; the most relevant papers either use 2026-era model families or make harness-level claims that are mostly model-independent. Older ReAct/tool-use citations are background, not evidence for current rates.

## Top Steals

1. **Tool-call-pair compaction:** retain recent full tool interactions, summarize evicted ones, and score by task completion plus token/runtime, not elegance.
2. **Executable subroutine tool:** when tool chains are deterministic, hide intermediate dataflow inside a bounded local block and return only the useful observation.
3. **Artifact-scored orchestration:** log intermediate artifacts and train/evaluate routing/review decisions from them; final-answer-only feedback is too sparse.
