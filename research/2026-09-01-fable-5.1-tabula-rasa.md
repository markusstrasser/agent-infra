# Fable 5.1 tabula rasa

**Verdict:** the model step is real but small for this harness; the harness itself is the problem. Fable 5.1 fixes none of the failure classes the architectural guards target (completeness exaggeration, unverified claims, approval-gate bypass, self-destroyed work all persist in the card), so the guard layer stays. What the new model *does* absolve is instruction mass, and that mass has re-accreted to 67K chars global + 70K project since the July slim, while Claude Code now ships the autonomy/scope/communication coaching in its own system prompt. The RSI machinery that is supposed to keep the harness honest has no consumer: 118 stale operator questions, 106 steward proposals, improvement-log silent since Aug 11, and the nightly test sentinel red for 23 consecutive runs on four trivially fixable tests. A sentinel without an actuator is theater; a question queue nobody drains is the flooding GOALS.md forbids. The cut list below targets those two, not the guards.

Verdicts:

- **Keep the BLOCK tier as is** (irreversible-write, destructive-git, bash-safety, invocation-discipline, dispatch-governance in `config/approval-tiers.json`), the two dispatchers, the completion Stop hooks, and the peer/worktree guards. §1 says why: the card shows these failure classes persisting or rising in 5.1. Zero fires on a guard is success, not decay.
- **The fleet violates constitution principle 3 ("measure before enforcing") for 79% of itself.** `hook-roi` and `hook-decay` can only see 44 hooks; every promote/demote decision made on that telemetry since June has been made on a fifth of the fleet. Fix at the altitude of the dispatchers: one outcome line per gate inside `pretool-bash-dispatch.py` `main()` instruments its 35 in-process gates in one hunk (drafted and positive-controlled in the follow-up patch; `pretool-universal-dispatch.py` already self-logs); the PostToolUse Write\|Edit fleet gets the same when it is consolidated into a dispatcher (which the July rule already requires at ≥5 hooks per matcher). Shared hooks → operator sign-off.
- **Stop writing what nothing reads.** ~190 MB of write-only telemetry, one file at 179 MB growing 1.4 MB a day. Retire the LOG hooks with no reader (`posttool-governance-state.py`, `session-stability-log.sh`, `permission-log.sh`, `posttool-l3-telemetry.sh`; the two skill-log hooks stay, their log has a reader) or give each a consumer in the same commit. This is the same disease as the question queue: generation without a named consumer.
- **Decide the 15 shadows.** Each is a measurement whose window has closed or never opens. `stop-unsupported-completion` is the one the 5.1 card argues FOR promoting (completeness exaggeration persists); the rest need a precision read of their shadow log and a promote/retire, not another window. Three expired: `stop-unsupported-completion`, `stop-stance-flip-shadow`, `sessionstart-compact-resume`. Four undecidable as written: `stop-answer-length-shadow`, `pretool-prebuild-inventory-shadow`, `stop-idle-lever-sweep`, `null-result-advisory`.
- **Cut the provably dead advisories** — corrected by the follow-up: cut 4 (`pretool-goal-drift.sh`, last fire 03-17; `stop-subagent-synthesis-gate.sh`, 06-19; `stop-plan-gate.sh`, 07-06; `permission-denied-retry.sh`, 04-04) and **hold 2** (`posttool-bash-failure-loop.sh`, `pretool-append-only-guard.sh`): both sit on the constitution's explicit fail-closed list, where a silent guard means the hazard is rare, not that the guard is dead. I had listed `bash-failure-loop` as a cut; the constitution's own text overrules that. The `spinning` predicate it was meant to pair with fired 44K times and was already unwired without cleaning its log. The change set exists: `hooks-cutlist.patch` removes 19 settings entries across 5 files and edits 6 scripts, JSON-validated, dispatcher parity 122/122 on the patched copies — not applied.
- **Remove the three double-wirings** in evals and arc-agi settings (the global dispatcher already runs them), and the duplicate dup-read counter.
- **Card-driven note, no build:** a PreToolUse regex is advisory against a model that rewrites commands to slip it (§6.2.1). The guards that must hold are the git pre-commit backstops (`just guard-doctor` verifies they are live); treat every PreToolUse BLOCK as defense in depth, not the gate.
