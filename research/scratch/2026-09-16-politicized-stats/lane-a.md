### Case 34.1 — US maternal mortality "doubling" is largely a death-certificate checkbox artifact — GROUNDED (A)
- **Number as used:** NVSS maternal mortality rate "nearly doubled, from 17.4 in 2018 to 32.9 per 100,000 live births in 2021."
- **Primary record:** Joseph KS, Lisonkova S, Boutin A, Muraca GM, Razaz N, John S, et al. "Maternal mortality in the United States: are the high and rising rates due to changes in obstetrical factors, maternal medical conditions, or maternal mortality surveillance?" *Am J Obstet Gynecol* 2024;230(4):440.e1-440.e13. doi:10.1016/j.ajog.2023.12.038 (published 2024-03-12; abstract read verbatim 2026-09-16).
- **The instrument:** NCHS recommended adding a **pregnancy checkbox** to the US death certificate in 2003; it identifies a decedent as pregnant/recently pregnant independent of whether pregnancy is named as a cause of death. Study covers all US deaths 1999-2021 under two identification rules: (1) NVSS methodology, including deaths identified **solely** by a positive checkbox; (2) alternative, requiring **at least one mention of pregnancy among the multiple causes of death**.
- **Measured magnitude (the strongest in this lane):** Verbatim — "Maternal deaths, per National Vital Statistics System methodology, increased by 144% (95% confidence interval, 130-159) from 9.65 in 1999-2002 (n=1550) to 23.6 per 100,000 live births in 2018-2021 (n=3489)… **38% of direct obstetrical deaths and 87% of indirect obstetrical deaths in 2018-2021 were identified because of a positive pregnancy checkbox**… Under the alternative formulation, the maternal mortality rate was **10.2 in 1999-2002 and 10.4 per 100,000 live births in 2018-2021**; deaths from direct obstetrical causes **decreased** from 7.05 to 5.82."
- **Smoking-gun sub-finding:** "maternal deaths with malignant neoplasms listed as a multiple cause of death increased **46-fold** from 0.03 in 1999-2002 to 1.42 per 100,000 live births" — cancer deaths in pregnant/postpartum women reclassified as maternal deaths.
- **Alternative construction and value:** +144% (official) vs **+2% (10.2 → 10.4), i.e. flat**, on the same underlying death records. The *entire* headline trend is instrument.
- **Direction:** The rising series is used by both US parties — as evidence for maternal-health appropriations and for abortion-access and racial-equity arguments, and (post-2022) by both sides of the *Dobbs* argument. Joseph et al. state the field context themselves: "Commentaries on maternal health in the United States highlight concerns related to the high and rising rates of maternal death." **Caveat, stated:** I did not this epoch reach a specific primary political document (e.g. the June 2022 White House *Blueprint for Addressing the Maternal Health Crisis*) quoting the 32.9 figure. The *construction* limb is grade A; the *political-use* limb is currently C (unquoted). Flagged as the one gap in this case.
- **Contest, recorded (both-directions inside the case):** ACOG and SMFM published a rebuttal and Joseph et al. replied — "Why improved surveillance is critical for reducing maternal deaths in the United States: a response to the American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists," *AJOG* 2024;231(2):e87-e92, doi:10.1016/j.ajog.2024.05.003, plus commentaries by Ahmed (e95), Deneux-Tharaux & Donati (e94: "Maternal mortality surveillance must go beyond death certificates"), Ness (e93). The checkbox-error finding is independently corroborated upstream by Catalano et al., *AJOG* 2019;222(3):269.e1 ("Pregnant? Validity of the pregnancy checkbox on death certificates in four states"). The *instrument* claim is not fringe; the *policy* reading of it is contested.

### Case 34.4 — "Unvaccinated" includes the first 14 days after the final dose — GROUNDED (A), but CONTRADICTED as usually stated
- **The definition, verbatim (CDC, grade A):** "a vaccine breakthrough case will be defined as a U.S. resident who has SARS-CoV-2 RNA or antigen detected on a respiratory specimen collected **≥14 days after completing the primary series** of an FDA-authorized SARS-CoV-2 vaccine. … A case will be excluded from further investigation if … the respiratory specimen that was positive … was collected **<14 days after completing the primary series**" — CDC, "Public health investigations of COVID-19 vaccine breakthrough cases: Case investigation protocol," archive.cdc.gov/www_cdc_gov/vaccines/covid-19/downloads/COVID-vaccine-breakthrough-case-investigations-Protocol.pdf. Same threshold in MMWR 70(21):e3 (2021-05-28): "a vaccine breakthrough infection is defined as the detection of SARS-CoV-2 RNA or antigen in a respiratory specimen collected from a person ≥14 days after receipt of all recommended doses." CDC's surveillance definitions page (archive.cdc.gov/…/php/hd-breakthrough.html) carries the same ≥14-day rule for primary series, monovalent booster and bivalent booster, each read verbatim 2026-09-16.
- **The mechanism:** the 14-day window assigns infections, hospitalizations and deaths occurring in the immediate post-dose period to the *not-fully-vaccinated* stratum. Because risk in that window is elevated (recent exposure, pre-existing incubating infection, and — during a rollout — a population being vaccinated precisely as incidence peaks), the rule mechanically inflates the "unvaccinated" numerator and shrinks the "vaccinated" one, which is the numerical basis for rate ratios used in 2021 messaging (e.g. the "pandemic of the unvaccinated" framing).
- **CONTRADICTED, and this is the finding:** the common political version of this lead — that CDC *counted the vaccinated as unvaccinated* — is **not** what the primary documents say. The CDC surveillance taxonomy has a distinct "**Partially vaccinated case**" category, and the protocol language is *exclusion from breakthrough investigation*, not reclassification as unvaccinated. Whether a given published rate-ratio chart collapsed "partially vaccinated" into "unvaccinated" is a **per-dashboard** question, decided by each state or CDC product's own technical notes, not by this definition. I did not this epoch reach a specific CDC or state dashboard technical note showing the collapse, so the strong form of the lead is **NOT FOUND / unproven**; the ≥14-day rule itself is GROUNDED grade A. Queries run: the definitional query above plus the archive.cdc.gov breakthrough pages. Next-epoch query in the final section.
- **Direction:** the definition served pro-vaccination messaging; the (overstated) critique served vaccine skeptics. Same evidence standard applied to both: the instrument fact is real and does bias the comparison; the accusation built on it overshoots what CDC's own documents say.
