### Case 34.1 — US maternal mortality "doubling" is largely a death-certificate checkbox artifact — GROUNDED (A)
- **Number as used:** NVSS maternal mortality rate "nearly doubled, from 17.4 in 2018 to 32.9 per 100,000 live births in 2021."
- **Primary record:** Joseph KS, Lisonkova S, Boutin A, Muraca GM, Razaz N, John S, et al. "Maternal mortality in the United States: are the high and rising rates due to changes in obstetrical factors, maternal medical conditions, or maternal mortality surveillance?" *Am J Obstet Gynecol* 2024;230(4):440.e1-440.e13. doi:10.1016/j.ajog.2023.12.038 (published 2024-03-12; abstract read verbatim 2026-09-16).
- **The instrument:** NCHS recommended adding a **pregnancy checkbox** to the US death certificate in 2003; it identifies a decedent as pregnant/recently pregnant independent of whether pregnancy is named as a cause of death. Study covers all US deaths 1999-2021 under two identification rules: (1) NVSS methodology, including deaths identified **solely** by a positive checkbox; (2) alternative, requiring **at least one mention of pregnancy among the multiple causes of death**.
- **Measured magnitude (the strongest in this lane):** Verbatim — "Maternal deaths, per National Vital Statistics System methodology, increased by 144% (95% confidence interval, 130-159) from 9.65 in 1999-2002 (n=1550) to 23.6 per 100,000 live births in 2018-2021 (n=3489)… **38% of direct obstetrical deaths and 87% of indirect obstetrical deaths in 2018-2021 were identified because of a positive pregnancy checkbox**… Under the alternative formulation, the maternal mortality rate was **10.2 in 1999-2002 and 10.4 per 100,000 live births in 2018-2021**; deaths from direct obstetrical causes **decreased** from 7.05 to 5.82."
- **Smoking-gun sub-finding:** "maternal deaths with malignant neoplasms listed as a multiple cause of death increased **46-fold** from 0.03 in 1999-2002 to 1.42 per 100,000 live births" — cancer deaths in pregnant/postpartum women reclassified as maternal deaths.
- **Alternative construction and value:** +144% (official) vs **+2% (10.2 → 10.4), i.e. flat**, on the same underlying death records. The *entire* headline trend is instrument.
- **Direction:** The rising series is used by both US parties — as evidence for maternal-health appropriations and for abortion-access and racial-equity arguments, and (post-2022) by both sides of the *Dobbs* argument. Joseph et al. state the field context themselves: "Commentaries on maternal health in the United States highlight concerns related to the high and rising rates of maternal death." **Caveat, stated:** I did not this epoch reach a specific primary political document (e.g. the June 2022 White House *Blueprint for Addressing the Maternal Health Crisis*) quoting the 32.9 figure. The *construction* limb is grade A; the *political-use* limb is currently C (unquoted). Flagged as the one gap in this case.
- **Contest, recorded (both-directions inside the case):** ACOG and SMFM published a rebuttal and Joseph et al. replied — "Why improved surveillance is critical for reducing maternal deaths in the United States: a response to the American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists," *AJOG* 2024;231(2):e87-e92, doi:10.1016/j.ajog.2024.05.003, plus commentaries by Ahmed (e95), Deneux-Tharaux & Donati (e94: "Maternal mortality surveillance must go beyond death certificates"), Ness (e93). ### Case 34.4 — "Unvaccinated" includes the first 14 days after the final dose — GROUNDED (A), but CONTRADICTED as usually stated
- **The definition, verbatim (CDC, grade A):** "a vaccine breakthrough case will be defined as a U.S. resident who has SARS-CoV-2 RNA or antigen detected on a respiratory specimen collected **≥14 days after completing the primary series** of an FDA-authorized SARS-CoV-2 vaccine. … A case will be excluded from further investigation if … the respiratory specimen that was positive … was collected **<14 days after completing the primary series**" — CDC, "Public health investigations of COVID-19 vaccine breakthrough cases: Case investigation protocol," archive.cdc.gov/www_cdc_gov/vaccines/covid-19/downloads/COVID-vaccine-breakthrough-case-investigations-Protocol.pdf. CDC's surveillance definitions page (archive.cdc.gov/…/php/hd-breakthrough.html) carries the same ≥14-day rule for primary series, monovalent booster and bivalent booster, each read verbatim 2026-09-16.

- **CONTRADICTED, and this is the finding:** the common political version of this lead — that CDC *counted the vaccinated as unvaccinated* — is **not** what the primary documents say. The CDC surveillance taxonomy has a distinct "**Partially vaccinated case**" category, and the protocol language is *exclusion from breakthrough investigation*, not reclassification as unvaccinated. Whether a given published rate-ratio chart collapsed "partially vaccinated" into "unvaccinated" is a **per-dashboard** question, decided by each state or CDC product's own technical notes, not by this definition. I did not this epoch reach a specific CDC or state dashboard technical note showing the collapse, so the strong form of the lead is **NOT FOUND / unproven**; the ≥14-day rule itself is GROUNDED grade A. ### Case 38.3 — Greece / ELSTAT: a statistics chief criminally prosecuted for an *accurate* figure — GROUNDED (A). The inverse-direction counterpart to the BLS case.
- **The number:** the Greek general-government deficit for **2009**, revised to **15.4% of GDP** and transmitted to Eurostat on **2010-11-10** by Andreas Georgiou, president of the Hellenic Statistical Authority (ELSTAT) 2010-08-02 to 2015-08-02. - **Primary record 1 — European Court of Human Rights (grade A).** *Georgiou v. Greece*, application no. **57378/18**, Third Section, judgment **2023-02-14** (judgment PDF read verbatim 2026-09-16). The Court's own recitation of the facts and of the Greek appellate verdict:
  > "§6. On 10 November 2010, the applicant transmitted revised data concerning the Greek deficit for the year 2009 to Eurostat. The applicant had not presented the data for approval to the seven-member administrative board of ELSTAT in advance. §7. The applicant claimed that his actions complied with the principle of professional independence in the European Statistics Code of Practice, **Principle 1.4 of which explicitly gave him 'sole responsibility' as head of the statistical authority, for the decision to release statistics.**"
  > "§9. On **6 December 2016** the applicant was **acquitted at first instance of all three charges** … §11.
