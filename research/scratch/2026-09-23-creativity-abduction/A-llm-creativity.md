**Verdict:** Keep the core of /brainstorm: denial, stratification, verbalized sampling (VS), and "fan-out is volume, not diversity". Nothing found in Jun–Sep 2026 refutes those parts for frontier models. Make three changes, each net-neutral or shorter in length:
1. Replace the synthesis ranking "novelty × feasibility" with a two-step selection: first gate on feasibility, then pick the most mutually distinct set. Self-rated novelty should not be trusted because of the documented LLM-judge "novelty mirage".
2. Add a standing ban on "bridge/unify/integrate existing approaches" framings. This is the measured default attractor for LLM research ideas.
3. Add one explicit "be creative/original, not merely effective" line to generation prompts.

Do not add more procedural scaffolding. For high-reasoning models, orchestration structure measurably stops mattering.

## Proposed skill edits (ranked by evidence strength)

1. **Synthesis: replace "ranked by novelty × feasibility" with "gate, then diversify"** (`synthesis-templates.md` line 105, SKILL.md Step 5). First gate each idea on feasibility and soundness; the pain-point gate from Step 5.5 is folded into this. Among the survivors, pick the largest set that is mutually distinct, in the spirit of IDEAgent's Yield or MMR-style selection, and order within that set by feasibility. Do not multiply by a self-rated novelty score. Evidence: #3 (novelty mirage, which gets worse under pairwise judging), #4–6 (judges are unreliable on quality and style-biased), #7 and #18 (gated set selection keeps about 99.9% of quality while raising diversity), #21.
2. **Assess novelty only against a reference.** When novelty must be judged, the reference set is the Step 2 dominant paradigms plus prior `.brainstorm/` artifacts, and the question is "differs from all of these on at least one core mechanism". Also ask one explicit question: "Is this narrow or source-bound?" Judges agree with experts only when asked this directly. Evidence: #3 (the explicit-narrowness test realigned judges), with the Scideator facet-delta definition as supporting context (SECONDARY).
3. **Pre-seed the denial cascade with the LLM research-idea attractor.** Denial round 1 always bans "connect/bridge literatures" and "integrate/unify/reconcile existing approaches", on top of the observed dominant paradigms. For the `blend` axis, add one line: a blend whose output is "unify A and B" is the default attractor, so record it as a dry cell. Evidence: #2 (47–64% vs 12% human; 22–39% vs 5%, holding across 9 LLMs).
4. **Add one explicit creativity-goal line to generation and dispatch payloads**, for example "Optimize for originality, not for the most effective or expected answer." This one sentence replaces nothing and costs about 15 tokens. Evidence: #1 (dz = +1.40 on 18 frontier models with quality preserved; four times the effect of turning on reasoning) and #15 (creative framing produced the largest originality effect on capable models).
5. **Qualify the Multi-Output default in `llmx-dispatch.md`.** Keep one generator producing many ideas for *diversity*. When the next step depends on quality-gated survivors, prefer sequential generation against compact signatures of prior ideas ("differ from these N one-line signatures") over one giant single-pass list. In the one-pass list, weak early ideas propagate. Evidence: #8 (One-Shot scored 0 Yield despite high diversity; frontier ideator; one study, so keep this as a softening, not a reversal).
6. **Keep worked example ideas out of generation prompts.** Keep the interface-design preset *directions*, but never paste sample *ideas* into the payload. Evidence: #19 (correct examples still cause pigeonholing) and #15 (a one-shot example did not raise originality). Low cost, moderate evidence (abstract only).
7. *(Optional, weakest)* If external fan-out is used for volume, give each parallel call a **randomly sampled ordinary persona** (e.g. "retired ferry mechanic", "municipal water engineer") instead of a neutral frame, and never an expert or famous-innovator persona. Evidence: #14 (GPT-4o only; the mechanism is plausibly independent of scale). The existing ban on expert personas stays.

Length constraint (Q4): edits 1, 3, 5 and 6 are rewrites or replacements of existing lines. Only edits 2, 4 and 7 add text, together about 5 lines. Evidence #12 shows that at high reasoning effort, orchestration structure stops mattering (o3-high range 0.023 vs 0.096 for GPT-4.1). The skill's many procedural steps are therefore probably ballast for Opus-class models, not a lever. No study directly shows that instruction length hurts creative output (#22). The honest position: extra scaffolding buys little, and there is no evidence it causes harm beyond examples and anchoring (#19).

## Rejected / not worth it

- **Adding a reasoning-effort or thinking-budget lever for diversity.** Reasoning gives a small originality gain (dz +0.34 [#1]; +3.4% for Qwen thinking [#15]) and cuts idea count by 38%. Keep effort high for quality, but it is not a diversity mechanism.
- **Temperature, min-p or decoding tweaks.** Higher temperature moves mass into invalid continuations [#16], and the prior memo found decoding interventions fail against semantic collapse. The skill is prompt-level anyway.
- **Reversing "single > MAS" based on 2605.17885 or 2608.09023.** Neither matches prompt conditioning or compute against the single agent, and the matched-conditioning result (ZQVnJXLMkR) still stands. Their positive finding is iterative generator–evaluator refinement, which is convergent work that `/critique` already owns. The same paper shows high-reasoning models do not need the structure [#12].
- **Importing IDEAgent, NOVA or parallel-tempering hypothesis search (2606.10587) as a loop.** These are heavy multi-round, multi-agent QD searches with LLM judges. Borrow the Yield selection objective (edit 1) and the signature memory (edit 5), not the machinery. The prior memo parked evolutionary ideation for the same reason.
- **An LLM-judge novelty score as a ranking signal.** Rejected by #3–#6. At most use it as a relative, reference-anchored check (edit 2).
- **Embedding-distance diversity metrics inside the skill.** Vendi and embedding metrics are valid for measuring diversity (87% or more agreement with experts [#17]), but computing them in the skill adds tooling for an operator-facing artifact that `matrix.json` already covers. Reconsider only if the skill gets an eval harness.
- **Training-side fixes** (CreativeInstruct 2608.07460, ReDiPO 2605.30021, multi-response training 2606.00544, SFT 2609.16454). These are out of scope for a prompt skill.
- **Analogical-retrieval systems** (2605.11258 analogical reasoning in science, 2603.19087 cross-domain mapping, TCA-SIR 2607.28498). These are ABSTRACT-ONLY and not read. They are consistent with the existing domain-forcing axis plus Exa knowledge injection, and nothing new is actionable without reading them in full.
