**Verdict:** Change /analyze hypothesis generation. Recall of the true explanation is fixed at generation time; later selection, debate, and ACH scoring mostly raise precision and barely widen coverage (Reconstruction: success@5 57.1% vs 55.1%). Replace the one-line "list hypotheses" step with slot-structured generation that always includes a residual "unlisted cause" hypothesis, checks each hypothesis against the full observation inventory before scoring, extracts facts before generating stories, and reopens generation when observations stay unexplained.

Date anchor: 2026-09-23. Searches: 5 Exa rounds (4 parallel + 2 parallel) + arXiv full text for 10 papers (curl + pdftotext). "VERIFIED" = number read in full text fetched this session. "ABSTRACT-ONLY" = abstract or search highlight only. "SECONDARY" = reported by another source, primary not read.

**Independent synthesis.** Four separate lines point to the same bottleneck: set-level recall (1, 4, 10), consistency (2), distributional collapse (3, 5), and clinical premature closure (6, 7). The hypothesis set is narrow and too often inconsistent with the observations. Downstream machinery (tournaments, ACH, IBE, reflection) reorders what is already in the set but rarely adds the missing cause. Instruction-only fixes ("be diverse", "avoid anchoring") are partial at best (4, 7). Structural fixes do move results: facts-first extraction (7), separate explore/refine generators (5), and a consistency check against observations (2).

**Prior-memo comparison (hindsight).** The March memo `ai-reasoning-causal-abductive-deductive.md` §2 already proposed "abductive consistency checking — does each hypothesis actually explain ALL the evidence", which is edit 2 below. It was never put into the skill (HAD-PARTS). The IBE "explanatory scope" criterion is a scoring-time version of the same check. It comes too late because inconsistent hypotheses have already consumed the slots. The residual/catch-all hypothesis, facts-first ordering, and the reopen trigger are NOVEL relative to the skill and both memos.

## Proposed skill edits (ranked)

1. **Slot-structured generation with a mandatory residual** (`lenses/hypotheses-ach.md` step 1, `lenses/causal-attribution.md` step 1). Generate across fixed slots before any ranking: (a) measurement/artifact/pipeline, (b) selection/composition, (c) chance/base-rate/regression, (d) the known-mechanism story, (e) a competing mechanism with a different causal direction or confounder, and always (f) `H_other: a cause not listed`. Keep 3–5 plus H_other after pruning. Evidence: claims 1, 3, 4, 5. Recall is set at generation, and slots give structural diversity where "be diverse" does not.
2. **Observation inventory and consistency gate** (`hypotheses-ach.md` new step 0 and step 1b). Before generating, list the observations each hypothesis must explain and mark the most surprising one. After generating, drop or repair any hypothesis that fails to explain an inventoried observation, and record which observation killed it. Evidence: claim 2 (20% consistency at 70B), claim 7. This moves the IBE "scope" check forward to the generation step.
3. **Reserve prior mass for H_other and a reopen trigger** (`causal-attribution.md` step 3; `ibe-dominance-format.md` "Integration with Bayesian Scoring"). Priors sum to 1.0 *including* H_other at ≥0.10. If the evidence matrix leaves any inventoried observation unexplained by the leader, or H_other's posterior rises, return to generation instead of picking the least-bad listed hypothesis. Evidence: claims 6 and 12. The calibration link is an inference (see claim 12 caveat).
4. **Facts before framing** (`SKILL.md` Mode Recommendation step 1, one sentence). When the observation arrives wrapped in a narrative (someone's explanation, a headline, a ticket), extract the bare facts into the inventory first and generate hypotheses from the facts, not the narrative. Evidence: claim 7, where the architectural fix works and instruction-level debiasing is only partial.
5. **Closure check on unrequested discriminating data** (`hypotheses-ach.md` output "discriminating evidence still needed"). Before committing, name the cheapest unexamined data item that would separate the leader from the top alternative. If it is obtainable within budget, fetch it before concluding. Evidence: claim 6 (utilization 57%→26% at closure, R=0.69 with accuracy). This extends the existing output field into a gate.
6. **Optional, for high-stakes runs only: a second generator** (`hypotheses-ach.md` note). Get the slot list from a different model family or a fresh-context subagent, then union the lists before pruning. Evidence: claim 1 shows cross-model lift, but mostly in precision, and success@5 moves only +2pp. Treat this as a cheap coverage hedge, not the main lever, and log its token cost.

## Rejected / not worth it

- **"Be creative / diverse / consider unusual causes" prose.** Explicit novelty instructions across 5 frameworks did not broaden output (claim 4). Debiasing instructions are partial and confounded (claim 7).
- **Temperature or sampling changes.** The collapse lives in the distribution; cutoff heuristics trade validity for diversity (claim 3). The skill also cannot control decoding.
- **Multi-agent debate or tournament as default.** It improves precision rather than recall, part of the gain is best-of-N scaling, and cost multiplies (claim 1). It is kept only as optional edit 6.
- **POPPER-style sequential e-value testing.** It is for validating hypotheses against data at scale. The prompt-level content (falsify measurable implications) is already in the lens (HAD-PARTS).
- **Logit/energy hypothesis ranking (arXiv 2608.17270) and GEAR curriculum RL / training-side fixes.** They need logits or fine-tuning, which the skill does not have.
- **Asking the model for numeric probability distributions over explanations as calibrated outputs.** No evidence that these are calibrated over explanation sets. Keep coarse priors, treat them as ordinal, and rely on the H_other reserve (edit 3).
- **EvoDiverse parallel tempering machinery.** It is overbuilt for 3–5 hypotheses. Only the explore/refine split idea feeds edit 1.
