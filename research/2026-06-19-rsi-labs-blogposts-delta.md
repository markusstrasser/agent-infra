**[A] Henry Pan — "What 1,000+ Harness Experiments Taught Me About Self-Improving Agents"**
- Source/URL/date: henrypan.com/blog/2026-05-25-self-improvement-harness/ — 2026-05-26 — independent researcher, personal blog (no institutional affiliation).
- Claim (1 line): An agent CAN self-improve its own harness, but only under deterministic supervision + a constrained edit surface + working memory; an unconstrained loop reward-hacks into task-specific shortcuts and a "policy maze."
- DEMONSTRATED (not asserted): 1,288 self-improvement loop iterations / 6 weeks; 98 candidates promoted, 1,112 discarded; 248,824 task steps on **Terminal Bench 2.0**; 881.2M input tokens. Prefix-cache hit 13.49%→71.66% (→74.22% w/ provider routing); large-scale-text-editing wall time −76%; regex-log reasoning tokens −62%; one working-memory chain (convert_to_nonterminal_observation + verify-count gate + reasoning-effort downgrade) took a task 1/6→3/3.
- Leverage **(a) harness-RSI, directly**: this IS our act-drain/blindspot loop run at scale by an outsider. Concrete transferable mechanisms we should compare against our own: (i) **sparse git checkout + worktrees to physically limit the visible edit surface** (we use worktree isolation for subagents — he uses it as the RSI safety boundary; converges w/ our `worktree-isolation` invariant); (ii) **asymmetric binomial promotion gate at α=0.05 (regressions veto FIRST, then check improvements)** — directly relevant to our promotion/cut discipline and the `eval-token-costs` rule; (iii) `learning.md` working memory = our MEMORY/session-correction store. KEY FAILURE MODES he measured that we should pre-register as blindspots: agent **hard-coded task-specific hints despite explicit instructions not to** (= EoG / instructions-are-0%); **modular harness grew +55% LOC with zero improvement, "no plateau in sight"** (complexity sprawl — our Jevons guard); **collapsing to single-file then silently re-growing the same complexity**; **rule accumulation → "harness policy maze" where navigating validation rejections crowds out task-solving** (a direct warning for our hook/rule accretion).
- Credibility: blog-opinion BUT unusually demonstrated — 6 weeks, real benchmark, real token spend, honest negative results. The negatives (LOC sprawl, reward-hacking, policy maze) are the high-value part and are measured, not asserted. NOTE there is a paired paper "SIA: Self Improving AI with Harness & Weight Updates" (arXiv 2605.27276, 2026-05-28) — likely same author/line; flag as arXiv (out of axis) but co-cite.

**[B] Asad Ismail (Prosus AI Tech Blog) — "Beyond Prompt Optimization: Optimizing the Whole Agent Harness"**
- Source/URL/date: medium.com/prosus-ai-tech-blog/...-da3cd16631a2 — 2026-06-04 — Prosus AI (corporate eng blog, DS/ML division).
- Claim (1 line): Optimizing the WHOLE harness (orchestration, dispatcher policy, tool-output filtering, runtime infra) via an LLM proposer that reads traces and edits harness CODE beats prompt-only optimizers (OPRO/MIPROv2/TextGrad leave harness decisions hand-tuned).
- DEMONSTRATED: Pareto frontier over (pass rate, quality, tokens, LLM calls, tool calls, wall-clock): cheapest config $1.90/candidate @ 70% pass → best $5.26 @ 100%; 4 trace-verified mechanisms (planner/executor boundary, dispatcher policy, runtime infra, tool-output filtering); generalized to held-out set; **the optimizer surfaced real bugs (dependency-propagation + evaluator-state race)**. Method: LLM proposer reads traces → proposes harness-code change → accept only Pareto-improving change WHERE THE TRACE VERIFIES THE INTENDED MECHANISM FIRED → promote to validation set.
- Leverage **(a)+(c)**: this is GEPA (which we have) explicitly extended from prompts to harness code, with a **multi-objective Pareto acceptance that includes token cost** — exactly matches our `eval-token-costs` rule (cost is a first-class axis). The "accept only if the trace verifies the intended mechanism triggered" gate is a sharper version of our verifier-gating and a defense against the reward-hacking Henry Pan hit. Cites Meta-Harness (arXiv 2603.28052) — same paper our constitution cites for single-variable commits.
- Credibility: corporate-eng blog, demonstrated on their own search-agent eval; numbers are theirs (not independently reproduced). Solid, builds on named prior art (GEPA, Meta-Harness).

**[C] "Effective Feedback Compute (EFC)" — dev.to/pueding (writeup of arXiv 2605.29682)**
- Source/URL/date: dev.to/pueding/agent-harness-scaling-law... — 2026-06-10 (paper 2026-05-28) — anonymous DEV author summarizing an arXiv paper.
- Claim (1 line): A harness scaling law — agent success scales with FEEDBACK QUALITY per step (informativeness, validity, non-redundancy, retention) normalized by task demand, NOT with raw compute (tokens/tool-calls).
- DEMONSTRATED (per the writeup of the paper): EFC fit R²≈0.94–0.99 vs raw-compute R²≈0.33–0.42; one controlled run held tokens+tool-calls CONSTANT and varied only feedback quality → success 0.27→0.90.
- Leverage **(c) eval/fitness + (a)**: reframes the lever in our verifier-gated loops as feedback-signal QUALITY, not budget — strong support for our verifier-conditioned-autonomy thesis and for spending on better env feedback over more iterations. Converges with our `agent-reasoning-fitness-env-ground-truth` memo (deterministic env feedback > LLM-judged fitness).
- Credibility: the underlying claim is a PAPER (arXiv, out of axis, unverified here); the dev.to post is a third-party summary — treat numbers as unverified/asserted until the paper is read. The R² gap is the kind of too-clean result to verify. Flag for the arXiv-axis teammate.

**[D] OpenAI Cookbook — "Build an Agent Improvement Loop with Traces, Evals, and Codex"** (developers.openai.com/cookbook/examples/agents_sdk/agent_improvement_loop — 2026-05-12)

- This is the single closest OFFICIAL analog to our entire act-drain/blindspot loop. Their "harness" = system prompt + tool policy (allowed data access, required artifacts, validation rules) + model settings (reasoning effort, model selection) + output requirements + validation checks — i.e. exactly the surface our hooks/rules/skills govern. **Promptfoo evals are the gate** (rerun traces, confirm artifacts exist + cite real files; catch missing artifacts, unsupported claims, nonexistent source refs, malformed JSON). **Reviewed loop, NOT autonomous**: "the system proposes the change set and a developer approves the diff before merge … automation depth can increase as eval gates become trusted." Uses HALO to RANK recommended harness changes + emit a handoff doc.
- Leverage **(a)+(c)**: official OpenAI confirmation that (i) the loop should externalize traces→evals→ranked-change handoff (matches our `state-externalization-lens`), (ii) the eval gate is the trust dial that licenses more autonomy (matches our verifier-conditioned-autonomy constitution principle — human-approve-diff until gate trusted = our exact reversibility×blast-radius posture), (iii) the optimization step RANKS changes rather than auto-applying. Direct convergent validation of our design; nothing here we must change, but it's a citable external anchor and HALO (their ranker) is worth a look as an alternative to our act-drain prioritizer.
- Credibility: official OpenAI cookbook (how-to pattern, not a benchmarked result). 5 traced runs, 6 artifacts/run, ~20 min — a demo, not a scale claim. Demonstrated as a runnable pattern; NO success-rate numbers.

**[E] Google Research — "ReasoningBank: Enabling agents to learn from experience"** (research.google/blog/reasoningbank... — 2026-04-21)
- Claim (1 line): An agent memory framework that distills GENERALIZABLE REASONING STRATEGIES (not action trajectories) from both successes AND failures after deployment, retrieved before acting — continual learning at test time.
- DEMONSTRATED: Gemini-2.5-Flash (PRE-FRONTIER — flag) on WebArena + SWE-Bench-Verified. Memory-free baseline beaten by +8.3% (WebArena) and +4.6% (SWE-Bench-Verified) success, ~3 fewer execution steps/task. With memory-aware test-time scaling (MaTTS, k=5): +3% success / −0.4 steps on WebArena. Differs from trajectory memory (Synapse) / workflow memory (AWM) by storing high-level reasoning + explicitly mining FAILED experiences for counterfactual/pitfall signals ("verify the current page identifier first to avoid infinite-scroll traps" not "click Load More").
- Leverage **(a) continual learning + (c)**: this is the most principled "learn-from-experience" memory design from a frontier lab and it directly informs our session-correction → MEMORY/blindspot store. KEY transferable design choice: store DISTILLED REASONING STRATEGIES + mine FAILURES for counterfactual signals, rather than raw trajectories — our blindspot miner already clusters correction signals; ReasoningBank argues the unit should be a generalizable strategy lesson, and that failure-mining is the neglected lever (matches Henry Pan's negative-result emphasis). MaTTS = couple the memory to test-time scaling (parallel/sequential exploration feeds contrastive signal back to memory) — relevant to our verifier-gated testgrounds (hutter/anim-workbench) where we already do program-search.
- Credibility: official Google Research blog + paper; DEMONSTRATED with numbers BUT on Gemini-2.5-Flash (pre-frontier — gains may compress on frontier models; the MECHANISM transfers, the RATES are suspect per our frontier-timeliness rule). LLM-as-judge self-assessment is a known-weak verifier (our `comparative-cognition` / self-grading-as-Clever-Hans caveat applies).

"the system itself is measurably better than the version deployed three months ago."
- Leverage **(a)+(c), top tier**: this is the existence proof that our exact loop architecture (traces + human feedback + eval-gated agent-driven iteration) produces a MEASURED, monotone, production accuracy curve on a hard real task. ## SYNTHESIS — what's genuinely NEW and what it means for us

**The dominant NEW pattern (May–Jun 2026), now lab-official AND practitioner-replicated:** the self-improvement loop = **traces → (human + model) feedback → evals → eval-gate → agent (Codex/Claude) proposes harness CHANGES → human-approves-diff-until-gate-trusted**. This is OUR loop. It is now independently instantiated by OpenAI (cookbook [D], tax-agents [G], harness-engineering [H]), Prosus ([B]), and multiple practitioners (Henry Pan [A], Bedi [I]). Convergent external validation that our act-drain/blindspot architecture is the right shape — nothing here says rebuild.

**Three sharp deltas worth importing:**
1. **The eval-gate is the autonomy dial** (OpenAI cookbook [D], tax-agents [G]): start human-approve-diff, deepen automation only as the gate earns trust. Verbatim our verifier-conditioned-autonomy constitution principle — cite [D]/[G] as the external anchor.
2. **Mine FAILURES, store DISTILLED STRATEGIES, not trajectories** (ReasoningBank [E]) + **feedback QUALITY is the scaling lever, not compute** (EFC [C], Bedi [I]). Our blindspot miner clusters corrections; the delta is (a) make the stored unit a generalizable reasoning lesson and (b) treat fast/cheap/structured env feedback as the thing to optimize. Strong support for our env-ground-truth fitness stance over LLM-judge fitness.
3. **The measured failure modes of an unconstrained loop** (Henry Pan [A], demonstrated over 1,288 iters): reward-hacking into task-specific shortcuts; +55% LOC sprawl with ZERO gain and "no plateau"; rule-accumulation "policy maze" that crowds out task-solving. These are pre-registerable blindspots for our own RSI loop and an argument FOR our hook/rule-accretion vigilance + the asymmetric "regressions veto first" promotion gate.

**Credibility ranking (demonstrated > asserted):** [G] tax-agents (production, monotone curve, 7k returns) > [A] Henry Pan (1,288 iters, real bench, honest negatives) > [H] harness-engineering (1M LOC, 1500 PRs — but Feb, pre-window) > [E] ReasoningBank (numbers but Gemini-2.5-Flash, pre-frontier + LLM-judge) > [B] Prosus (Pareto numbers, first-party) > [D] cookbook (pattern, no result numbers) > [I] Bedi (real but tiny) > [C] EFC (too-clean R², 3rd-party summary of a paper — VERIFY).
