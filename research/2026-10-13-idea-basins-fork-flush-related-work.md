**Verdict:** Fork-and-Flush (F&F, arXiv 2610.07447) has one strong independent replication of its core claim and two qualifiers. The replication is SLA (2610.07625, one day later): stateless agents that rebuild a fresh context from state the harness owns beat three long-session frameworks at up to 1B tokens. Elo-per-token (2609.15309) adds the budget-splitting result: after a few compactions, coding agents scale no better than independent sampling, and three parallel sessions beat one 100M-token session by +264 Elo. The qualifiers are FML-bench (2605.17373) and Loreley (2608.19703). When improvements are dense, greedy or sequential search matches or beats broader search; that includes Zstandard compression, where Sequential Champion had the best mean of three policies over 1,008 jobs. Diversity alone does not predict outcomes (FML-bench, 2604.19440), while prompting for ideation diversity does help (2511.15593). So for our loops, trigger fork-and-flush on stagnation rather than on a fixed schedule, and only where a trusted verifier does the selection. As of 2026-10-13, F&F has 0 citations (S2) and no academic follow-up or critique; only press coverage.

## What transfers to our loops

1. **hutter Grinder and fleet beam-fork.** `hutter/LOOP.md` already forks: it pins parent A and fans N single-variable deltas across boxes, while the ratchet stays serial. That is fork without flush, F&F's weakest positive arm (fork-only 0.57 vs both 0.71, from the parent's read of F&F). SLA's Worker-isolation ablation (A1) shows the cost of carried context compounding over a run. The change worth testing is to start a fresh grinder session at a plateau, seeded only from the ledger and ratchet head. The counter-evidence argues for a plateau trigger over F&F's fixed 0/33/66% schedule. Loreley (C4) found Sequential Champion best on Zstandard compression at 48 jobs, and FML-bench (C3) found greedy wins while improvements are dense and switch-on-stagnation wins overall. LOOP.md's existing plateau clause is the right hook point.
2. **hutter Dreamer.** SLA's Advisor (A1) is a Dreamer analogue: stateless, reading a harness-built evidence summary, assigning experiments, costing <0.6% of tokens, and lowering progress when removed. The steal is to make every Dreamer epoch a new session that reads a ledger-derived summary separating "measured non-improvement" from "inconclusive failure". `DREAMER-BOOT.md:51` already starts fresh sessions for cron recreation, so this is HAD-PARTS. C7 adds a caution: one implementation's flat result should not mark an idea family "flat". LOOP.md's calibration map does mark families flat; requiring 2+ implementations before retiring a family is the fix suggested by C7.
3. **agent-infra /loop and the wakeup-cadence escalation (stale_count ≥2 → change frame; ≥4 → HUMAN.md).** A2 and A1 give a concrete frame change for stale_count ≥2 when a clear verifier exists: fork K fresh-context sessions from on-disk state, give each a fixed slice, and keep the verifier-best. A2's inflection rule suggests the per-session budget. This does not transfer to principal-verifier or self-judged loops. B4 shows self-judged loops become accept-all (56% of claimed gains were ≤0), so escalation stays the rule there.
4. **brainstorm "parallel fan-out is diversity-negative" (`skills/brainstorm/SKILL.md:99,171`).** None of the evidence here contradicts it, and none tests it directly. F&F's diversity comes from discarding conversational commitment plus inherited artifacts plus grader selection, not from parallelism. The parent's read of F&F has best-of-4 from scratch at 0.54 vs single 0.47; fork-only 0.57; flush+fork 0.71. ArcticSwarm (C5) shows peer reads cause early consensus and isolation recovers +3.8 points, which fits brainstorm's "don't expect agent count to diversify". C1 shows *prompting* for diversity has a causal effect (+6.9/+8.4 medal points), which fits brainstorm's prompt-structure mechanism. C3 and D1 show unguided diversity does not predict outcomes. Brainstorm has no verifier, so F&F's selection step has nothing to act on there. No edit is warranted.
