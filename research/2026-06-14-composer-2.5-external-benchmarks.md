# Composer 2.5 — External Benchmarks vs Our Internal Findings

**Date:** 2026-06-14
**Question:** Do published/external benchmarks corroborate our 5 internal measurements of Cursor Composer 2.5 (Kimi K2.5 base, Cursor RL-tuned)?
**Bottom line:** External data is **all coding-agent benchmarks + vendor marketing**. It corroborates our *positioning* claims (#1 frontier-equal review-class, #4 cheap/fast non-reasoning coder, #5 latency-is-agent-overhead) but is **silent on the faithfulness/over-extraction findings (#2, #3)** that are our actual differentiators — no external eval measures structured-extraction precision or hallucination for this model. Treat Cursor's own numbers as vendor self-report; the one independent source (Artificial Analysis) ranks it **3rd, just below** Opus/GPT-5.5, which matches our "frontier-equal-ish, not frontier-leading" read.

## Per-source detail

### 1. Cursor official — cursor.com/blog/composer-2-5  ·  Authority: **C (vendor self-report)**
- Built on Moonshot **Kimi K2.5** (same checkpoint as Composer 2). MoE ~1T total / ~32B active.
- Pricing: **\$0.50/M in, \$2.50/M out** (standard); **\$3.00/\$15.00** (fast, "same intelligence", lower than rivals' fast tiers).
- "25× more synthetic tasks than Composer 2"; 85% of compute on Cursor's own post-training (RL + continued pretrain + localized text-feedback technique).
- Self-reported benchmarks (treat skeptically — vendor picks the board):
  - **SWE-Bench Multilingual 79.8%** (Opus 4.7 80.5%, GPT-5.5 77.8%)
  - **Terminal-Bench 2.0 69.3%** (Opus 4.7 69.4%, **GPT-5.5 82.7%** — Composer clearly behind here)
  - **CursorBench v3.1 63.2%** (in-house; HN flagged as non-independent)
- Marketing themes that corroborate #3 obliquely: "better effort calibration", "follows complex instructions more reliably" = admission prior gen over-generated/over-worked.

### 2. Artificial Analysis — Coding Agent Index  ·  Authority: **A (independent third-party, the strongest external source)**
- Composer 2.5 = **score 62, ranked 3rd**, +14 over Composer 2.
- Above it: Opus 4.7 max in Claude Code (66), GPT-5.5 xhigh in Codex (65) — "marginally higher but 10–60× the cost."
- Cost/task: **\$0.07 (std), \$0.44 (fast)**. Task time: **9.3 min std / 6.7 min fast** (3rd-fastest agent).
- Improvements cited: +35 pts SWE-Bench-Pro-Hard-AA (12%→47%), gains on Terminal-Bench v2, SWE-Atlas-QnA.
- Published 2026-05-21. **This is the load-bearing independent corroboration for #1 and #4.**

### 3. Kimi K2.5 base model — huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K2.5  ·  Authority: **B (vendor of base model, but the inherited ceiling)**

- Strong coding + reasoning ceiling. **No published hallucination/faithfulness/IFEval number** → the inherited capability profile cannot confirm or deny our #2/#3. Cursor's RL further shifts behavior away from base.

### 4. The New Stack / The Decoder / HN  ·  Authority: **C (secondary press + anecdote)**

### 5. Cursor blog (Composer 1) — speed methodology  ·  Authority: **C**
- "4× faster generation than similar models" (raw TPS, normalized to Anthropic tokenizer). Confirms the *raw* model is fast, reconciling with our slow *per-call doc* latency = agent/prompt overhead, not model speed.

## What external data CANNOT tell us

1. **Findings #2 and #3 are our moat.** No public benchmark measures structured-extraction faithfulness, unsupported-claim rate, or contract-gated discipline for Composer 2.5 or Kimi K2.5. Our biomedical-9%-spurious / financial-35%-unsupported / 2×-GPT-5.5-volume numbers have **zero external corroboration or contradiction** — they are net-new signal. Keep them; they're why we ran our own eval.
2. **Composer 2.5 is largely absent from neutral leaderboards.** It appears on Artificial Analysis (1 independent source) but NOT independently on SWE-bench Verified leaderboard, Aider polyglot, LiveCodeBench, or LMArena/Copilot Arena as a distinct entry — expected for a vendor-specific RL'd model you can't run outside Cursor. **All SWE/Terminal-Bench numbers above are Cursor self-reported**, not independently reproduced. AA's index is the only third-party measurement.
3. **Defect-review parity (#1) is adjacent, not identical.** External evidence shows agentic-coding near-parity; it does not measure injected-defect review specifically. Transfer is plausible, not proven externally.
4. **The faithfulness-vs-GPT-5.5 precision gap (#3) is unmeasured externally.** Only an over-generation *anecdote* exists. Our controlled comparison is the only real evidence.

**Skeptic's note:** Cursor's blog cherry-picks boards where it wins (SWE-Multilingual, CursorBench) and is quiet on Terminal-Bench 2.0 where GPT-5.5 leads 82.7 vs 69.3. The independent AA ranking (3rd, below frontier) is the honest read and aligns with our "high-recall generator that needs a verifier, not a frontier reasoner" framing.
