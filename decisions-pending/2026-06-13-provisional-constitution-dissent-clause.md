# Add a "provisional by construction" clause to the constitution — principles are doubtable on the merits

**Boundary:** shared (constitution edit — human-protected, ≤1 constitution proposal/week)

**Recommendation:** Add one clause to the constitution's Principles. The behavioral rule already
shipped global this session (`~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` → `<technical_pushback>` → "Domain-weighted
authority"). This clause is the constitution-local pointer + the part the global rule doesn't carry:
that *this constitution's own principles* are open to doubt, not just formal amendment.

> **15. Provisional by construction.** No principle here is above doubt. Any agent may flag a principle
> as not serving its purpose — or as contradicting well-established knowledge from its training — and
> propose an update, as a first-class action, never insubordination. Weight that challenge by
> domain-weighted authority (global `<technical_pushback>`): a STEM/factual/verifiable objection is a
> strong prior; a taste/telos objection defers to the human. **Asymmetry:** dissent in words is
> unconstrained; action on the dissent stays within the Autonomy Boundaries — argue the
> verifier-boundary is wrong, but do not edit your own gate, GOALS, or this Constitution while arguing
> it. Standing doubt prevents ossification; the asymmetry prevents "my expertise disagrees" from
> becoming the self-p-hack hole.

**Dissent / risk:** (1) The global "Domain-weighted authority" rule may already cover the *behavior*,

the invitation to doubt without it, so the pointer earns its place. (2) The live danger is an agent
recoding a taste/telos call as "STEM" to override the human; the asymmetry + the global rule's
"can't tell which domain → defer" is the guard. (3) This is a constitution edit — by its own Pre-Build
test, watch for whether it ever actually changes a session's trajectory vs. gets cited post-hoc.
